Ram D250 Engine Oil Change (sample)

Warm the engine, then drain the oil into a pan by removing the drain plug. Replace the
oil filter and lightly coat the new gasket with clean oil. Refill with 5W-30 or 10W-30
engine oil, run the engine, then check the dipstick and top up as needed.

Change oil and filter every 3,000 miles under severe service such as towing or short trips.
