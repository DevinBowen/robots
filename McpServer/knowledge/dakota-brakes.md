# Dakota Brake Service (sample)

To bleed the brakes, start at the wheel farthest from the master cylinder (right rear),
then left rear, right front, and left front. Use DOT 3 brake fluid only. Keep the master
cylinder reservoir topped up during bleeding so air is not drawn into the system.

Replace brake pads when the lining is thinner than 1/8 inch (3 mm). Inspect rotors for
scoring and measure runout; machine or replace them if beyond specification.
